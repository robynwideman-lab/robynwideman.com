Stoneblood Saga

They tried to hide him. They tried to protect him. Nathan Stoneblood was never going to stay hidden.

A boy raised in a quiet village, a bloodline the powerful would kill to control, and a kingdom sliding toward war. Nathan Stoneblood gathers friends, masters magic he was never meant to have, and builds Elderwood from a refuge into a power. Eight books of classic sword-and-sorcery with a progression backbone.

Readers of Riyria, The Wandering Inn (without the word count) and Cradle. 4.3 average across 6,000+ Goodreads ratings.
