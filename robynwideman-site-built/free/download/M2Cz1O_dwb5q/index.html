Send the EPUB to your Kindle

Amazon accepts EPUB now. Easiest route: open amazon.com/sendtokindle, sign in, and drop the EPUB file on the page. It appears in your library on every Kindle device and app within a few minutes.

Or email the EPUB as an attachment to your Kindle address (find it under Amazon → Content & Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings).
